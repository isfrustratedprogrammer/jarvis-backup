Privacy policy

jarvis-backup is a personal backup tool. Its only user is its owner, who runs it on their own server.

  • What it accesses: with the drive.file scope, only the files and folders the tool itself creates in the owner's Google Drive.
  • What it stores: encrypted backup files (restic repository) in the owner's own Google Drive. The encryption key stays on the owner's server.
  • Sharing: no data is collected, sold or shared with anyone. There are no analytics, ads or third parties.
  • Google user data: it is used only to store and restore the owner's backups, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
